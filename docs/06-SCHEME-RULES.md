Examples: ₹500 × 11 = ₹5,500 + ₹500 benefit = ₹6,000. ₹1,000 × 11 =
₹11,000 + ₹1,000 = ₹12,000. ₹1,500 × 11 = ₹16,500 + ₹1,500 = ₹18,000.

These are initial configurations, not hard-coded logic. Version schemes
and snapshot accepted terms at enrollment. Never recalculate historical
entitlements from a newly edited scheme. Award benefit only after
backend verification of all qualifying payments and configured
conditions.
