# Product Requirements

Roles: Customer, Store Staff, Accountant, Admin, Super Admin.

Customer: registration, OTP/email verification, profile, KYC, scheme
catalog/details, enrollment, installment calendar, Razorpay payment,
payment history, receipts, notifications, support, completion and
redemption.

Admin: KPIs, customers, KYC queue, scheme CRUD/versioning, enrollments,
installments, payments, reconciliation, optional controlled offline
payments, refunds, completed schemes, redemptions, reports,
notifications, banners, support, stores, settings and audit logs.

Configurable policies: installment count, monthly amount, benefit,
joining window, due date, grace period, missed payments, cancellation
and redemption.
