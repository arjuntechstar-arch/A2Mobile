# Notifications & Support

Use Firebase Cloud Messaging. Optional SMS/email through configured
providers. Notify for due reminders, payment success/failure, KYC,
activation, completion and redemption.

Reminder schedule is configurable (for example 7/3/1 days before, due
date and overdue).

Provide notification center with read state/deep links plus FAQ, store
contact, configurable WhatsApp/contact options and support tickets with
Open/In Progress/Resolved/Closed states.
