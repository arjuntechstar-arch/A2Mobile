# Mobile App

Bottom navigation: Home \| Schemes \| Payments \| Notifications \|
Profile.

Screens: Splash, onboarding, register, phone OTP, email verification,
login, forgot password, home, available schemes, scheme details, join
wizard, KYC, address/nominee if required, terms, active scheme,
installment calendar, payment checkout/status, transactions, receipt,
completion, redemption, notifications, profile, support, FAQ,
terms/privacy.

Home shows active plan, amount paid, installments completed, next due
amount/date, total contribution, shop benefit and expected eligible
purchase/redemption value.
