# Reporting & Accounting

Reports: daily/monthly collections, scheme-wise collections, customer
payments, pending/overdue installments, KYC, completed schemes, benefit
liability, redemptions, refunds and reconciliation.

Keep customer contributions, shop-funded benefits, gateway fees,
refunds, redemptions and product invoices conceptually separate. Do not
invent tax/accounting treatment; make receipt/invoice behavior
configurable for professional review.
